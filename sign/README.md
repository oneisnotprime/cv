# Sign of the Times

A pixel-art sign-spinning combo game, Tony Hawk style, with a store behind you that keeps hiring.
Open `index.html` in a browser (no build step).

- **Tricks**: `J/Z` spin, `K/X` twirl, `L/C` toss (press again to catch). Hold `↑`/`↓` while pressing for the bigger variant (Overhead 360, Low Sweep, Helicopter, Leg Pass, Moon Toss, Behind the Back). `J` mid-toss for a 360 Under.
- **Combos**: press the next trick as the current one ends. The gold zone on the timing bar is PERFECT (1.5x); too early is a FUMBLE. A combo is worth its points × trick count, and it banks when you stop.
- **Greed**: every trick in a combo makes the balance wobble harder (`←/→`, needle over your head) and the timing windows tighter. Repeated tricks are worth less. Drop the sign and the combo is gone.
- **Special** (`Space`): fills from perfects and banked combos. While full, every trick is worth 1.5x.
- **Audience**: cars stopped at the red light multiply banked combos. Big combos get honks and tips.
- **Story**: 8 shifts with rising quotas. Pay buys normal upgrades in the back office. The GLADCO Wellness Program offers free, permanent, much stronger enhancements (time slow, fixed smile, recalibrated inner ear, tendon lattice, sleep deferral, brand lens, full integration). They change how you look, what your phone shows you, and which of the 3 endings you can reach.
- Free Spin (90 s score attack) unlocks after any ending. `P`/`Esc` pause, `M` mute. Touch controls appear on phones.
