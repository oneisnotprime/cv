# Diarrhea Blasters

A two-player cooperative platformer. Open `index.html` in a browser (no build step).

- **Master** (player 1) has to get from the start of each stage to the Golden Throne. He can walk and make small jumps, and that's it. `A`/`D` walk, `W` or `Space` jump, `S` + jump drops through planks.
- **Blaster** (player 2) flies and gets Master over everything else with his powers of blasting diarrhea. Arrows fly; hold `/` (or `Right Shift`, `L`) to blast, and while blasting the arrows aim. Hold `.` (or `Enter`, `K`) to charge a **Mega Blast**.
- The stream **pushes Master** (lift him up walls, shove him across gaps), **crusts into temporary platforms** where it lands (bridges, steps, spike covers; crust crumbles after 8 s), **dissolves toilet paper walls**, **puts out fires**, **knocks plungers around**, and **fills toilet bowls**, which open the matching colored doors.
- The Mega Blast launches Master, splats a big chunk of crust, and destroys plungers. Firing one next to Master is a rocket jump.
- Blaster's tank drains while blasting and refills when he rests. Burritos refill it and make him spicy (free blasting for 8 s).
- 7 stages. Porta-potties are checkpoints. `R` respawns, `Esc`/`P` pauses, `M` mutes. Gamepads work: the first pad is Master, the second is Blaster.
