# Last Word: word and property data

Design data only; no game code yet. Everything in `data/` is generated by `tools/`.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data/lexicon.tsv` | Every word with a game role, one row per word under its most useful role |
| `data/inert.tsv` | Words with no role yet, kept with their WordNet category |
| `data/proper.tsv` | Names of people and places (excluded) |
| `data/overrides.tsv` | Hand rulings on a word's role and archetype; these win over the automatic choice |
| `data/inflections.tsv` | Comparatives and superlatives of common adjectives, with their base word |
| `data/demo.json` | Property values for the 351-word demo dictionary, built from `tools/demo_words.txt` |
| `data/demo_candidates.tsv` | Short, very common words to hand-pick a demo dictionary from |
| `data/properties.json` | The property schema: id, group, type, default, meaning |
| `data/archetypes.json` | Each class and archetype, its word count and the property groups it fills |
| `data/letters.json` | All 26 letters: class, source, share of letters across the lexicon |
| `data/operators.json` | Adjective operations and the seed list of absolute adjectives |
| `data/watchlist.tsv` | Words whose meaning is strong for their spelling cost, ranked by cost within each reason |
| `data/stats.json` | Counts by role, class, archetype and frequency tier |

## Columns in the word lists

`word`, `role` (noun, verb, adjective, adverb), `class`, `archetype`, `zipf` (frequency, 0 to 8;
higher is more common), `tier` (core, common, uncommon, rare, obscure), `synset` (WordNet id of
the meaning used), `other_roles` (roles the word could have had).

## How a word gets its role and class

- Each word has one role. The choice weighs how often the word is used that way against how
  useful the role is in play (objects first, then events, then modifiers, then verbs).
- Nouns take the archetype of their nearest WordNet ancestor on the anchor list in
  `tools/build_lexicon.py`, falling back to the WordNet category. The meaning used is the first
  of the word's three most common senses that has a role.
- Acts and events (war, theft, fire) are the `event` class: words dropped on a target or area
  that make something happen.
- `overrides.tsv` corrects any word by hand. Every demo word is pinned there.

## Property values

`tools/demo_words.txt` is the hand-written source. `p=v` sets the word's own property. `p*x` and
`p+x` change that property on whatever the word modifies: an adjective's noun, a verb's caster,
or the things inside an aura, weather or event. Properties not listed stay at their default.

Comparatives and superlatives reuse the base adjective's operations at a higher degree
(`operators.json`): a multiplier `f` becomes `f ** degree`, an added amount `a` becomes
`a * degree`.

## Rebuilding

```
npm i wordnet-db            # WordNet dict files
pip install wordfreq
tools/build_all.sh node_modules/wordnet-db/dict
```
